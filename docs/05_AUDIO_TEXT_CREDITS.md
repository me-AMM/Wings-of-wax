# 05 Audio, text, credits and README

## Audio (ambience only, no music)
46 sounds: 43 effects + 3 ambience loops. The owner makes the files with jsfxr (WAV) and drops them in audio/sfx/ and audio/ambience/. Wire them BY FILE NAME through Audio.play("name"). A missing file must be skipped silently.

Buses: SFX, Outdoor (SFX with a low-pass filter; used for everything on missions and outside, because the Moon is silent and the sound is "heard through the suit"), Ambience. Mute toggle on key M; never let a sound be the only signal (alarms also have icon/text). The storm alarm is the loudest sound; steps and hums the quietest.

**All ambience stops (full silence) when a storm warning starts, so only the alarm plays; it returns when the storm ends.**

Priority 1 (wire first): step.wav (each step, vary pitch slightly), interact_done.wav (hold-E action done), hurt.wav (Health drops; also out-of-suit-oxygen), lander_touchdown.wav (cargo lander and crew lander), sample_pickup.wav (sample or cargo collected), storm_warning.wav (loops during countdown), storm_hit.wav (countdown reaches zero), ui_click.wav (any button incl. Judge mode), success.wav (success ending), fail.wav (failed ending).

Priority 2: interact_hold.wav (while holding E), clean_panel.wav (panel cleaned, with sparkle), pack_swap.wav, slider_tick.wav (slider enters a new zone, pitch by zone), harvest.wav, lab_process.wav, waypoint_reached.wav, ice_dig_hit.wav (marker stops in the green zone), suit_o2_low.wav (suit oxygen under 25%, repeats every 2 s), shelter_door.wav (enter the shelter), info_card.wav, warning_low.wav (pack under 10%, water under 12 L, oxygen low; must differ from the storm alarm), star.wav (each star on the ending, rising pitch).

Priority 3: eat.wav, drink.wav, sleep.wav, gym.wav, heal.wav, water_radish.wav, plant.wav, building_unlock.wav (locked building opens), power_down.wav (blackout starts), mission_start.wav, ice_dig_miss.wav, rare_stone.wav, countdown_tick.wav (each of the last 10 s, quiet, under the alarm), storm_clear.wav, shadow_start.wav (days 11, 25), sunrise.wav (days 13, 27), flashlight.wav, ui_hover.wav, notice.wav (HUD notice), day_start.wav (06:00).

Ambience loops (.ogg, Priority 3): suit_breath (outside and on missions; the most important one), hab_hum (near the habitat and buildings), recycler_hum (near the recycler when life support is medium or high).

Optional fallback (only after the core milestones, and only if audio/sfx is still empty): write `tools/make_sfx.py` (Python standard library: wave + math) that synthesizes simple 8-bit style WAVs for the Priority 1 sounds. Credit as "sound effects generated by a script during the event".
